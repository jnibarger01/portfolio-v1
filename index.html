      Behind the scene

      The world is built live in your browser with Three.js. The terrain is a 257×257 height field shared by the mesh, the physics, and a GPU grass shader. The grass is a wrapping patch of instanced blades that bend in the wind and around your tires. Trees are thousands of alpha-tested leaf cards, and the lake shader reads terrain depth for its shoreline foam.

      The car is the actual 2024 Toyota 4Runner TRD Pro GLB from Jace's Toyota Showroom project, Draco-compressed from 28 MiB to about 1.2 MiB.

      Sound is synthesized with Web Audio. Whispers and circuit times are stored by the small Node server that also renders every page's SEO content.

      Inspired by the interaction model of Bruno Simon's portfolio. The code, models, world, and copy here are original. Fonts: Amatic SC and Nunito (OFL).
